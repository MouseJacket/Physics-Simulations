# Physics Simulations
While I am actively working as a game developer, I hope to one day get a PHD in theoretical physics. I am currently working towards a minor in undergraduate physics. For this program, I was required to make a series of physics simulations in JupyterLabs using VPython. Here are a collection of some of my favorites.

## Alpha Centauri Vacation

This was a model of a Lorentz Transformation that I made. A ship, traveling from Earth to Alpha Centauri, moves close to the speed of light, and therefore experiences time and space dilation in accordance with general relativity. I chose to have some fun with this simulation and model my ship after the USS Enterprise.

## 3 Body Problem

This is a model of a 3 body problem that I created that graphs the gravitational kinetic and potential energy of three planets in unstable orbit, as well as the total energy of the system. The peaks in the graph are due to planets passing by one another.

![3 Body Problem](https://cdnb.artstation.com/p/assets/images/images/097/278/761/large/eamon-aveni-screenshot-2026-03-16-211608.webp?1773710430)

## Entropy Tracker

This program tracks two interacting Einstein models of atoms. The user can randomly assign new quanta to one of the 6 oscillators on each atom and see how the entropy of the system rises

![Entropy Tracker](https://cdna.artstation.com/p/assets/images/images/097/280/378/large/eamon-aveni-screenshot-2026-03-16-230855.webp?1773716965)

## Electric Field Simulator

This is a program that I created that dynamically integrates the electric field at an observation location of a variety of different charges, including a uniformly charged ring, disk, and capacitor. In this screenshot, it is integrating a negative disk.
