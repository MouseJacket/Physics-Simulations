# Physics Simulations
While I am actively working as a game developer, I hope to one day get a PHD in theoretical physics. I am currently working towards a minor in undergraduate physics. For this program, I was required to make a series of physics simulations in JupyterLabs using VPython. Here are a collection of some of my favorites.

## Alpha Centauri Vacation

This was a model of a Lorentz Transformation that I made. A ship, traveling from Earth to Alpha Centauri, moves close to the speed of light, and therefore experiences time and space dilation in accordance with general relativity. I chose to have some fun with this simulation and model my ship after the USS Enterprise.
